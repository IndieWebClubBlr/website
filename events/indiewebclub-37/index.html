Agenda

Let’s talk about slash pages (/pages) on our websites!

  • Slash pages are standard pages on your website, usually with well known slugs like /now, /about, or /uses. They tend to describe the individual behind the site and are a distinguishing characteristic of the IndieWeb.
  • We’ll begin with some examples of /pages we like from across the IndieWeb. What is the appeal of /pages? Is it the insight into somebody’s life? Or is it a discovery mechanism for new things, hobbies, and interests? What do you look for when you’re browsing somebody’s website? Bring your favorites!
  • What /pages would you like to have on your own website? Have people created their own /pages? How different is the process of creating/maintaining a /page from some other things on your website? How often do you update them?
  • Are there any pages you would like to add to your website, but something is stopping you? What is your biggest roadblock?
  • What slashpages would you like to see more of on the IndieWeb? What do you feel is missing from the list at slashpages.net?

We’ll spend the last hour of the meetup writing and publishing a piece on our personal websites. We’ll provide writing prompts to get you started.

Remember to bring a device you can use to write and post to your blog!
