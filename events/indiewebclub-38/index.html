Agenda

This will be a co-working and writing session. We’ll work on our personal websites, try new designs, build new features, squash bugs, polish old drafts, or do some long-neglected digital housekeeping.

If you don’t have any programming or design experience, don’t worry! We’ll help you with any questions you might have participating on the IndieWeb, no matter your level of technical experience. If possible, we’ll pair you up with a buddy who can help you out with the technical and design side of web development.

Bring:

  1. Your laptop (+ charger)
  2. Ideas for new features/designs you want to add to your website (or just a list of enhancements you want to make)
  3. If you’re new to programming or design, come with questions so we can find you answers!

As usual, we will end the meetup with a writing prompt and a focused writing session.

Important

  2. The cost of the event will be ₹300, to be paid to the venue when you arrive.
  3. Outside food and drink are not allowed at the venue.
  4. We might have limited desk space at this venue, so we’ll have to get creative!
  5. We only have 5 or 6 plug points at this venue, so please make sure your devices are charged.
