IndieWebClub BLR Guidelines

IndieWebClub BLR (IWCB) is a non-commercial community space. Self-promotion of your personal blogs and IndieWeb projects is allowed and encouraged, as long as they adhere to the following guidelines.

If any of these guidelines are not clear, please ask one of the moderators. Violating the guidelines will at first result in a warning via private message. Violating the guidelines repeatedly may result in a ban from the WhatsApp group and the community space.

Engagement Guidelines

Dos

  • The IWCB group is intended to be a high-value, low-spam extension of our in-person meetup space.
  • Post about blogs you’ve read on the IndieWeb.
  • Ask questions about your participation on the IndieWeb.
  • Ask about concepts or topics you may not understand, even if those questions might sound dumb. You might be helping somebody else by asking a question!

Don’ts

  • Be mindful of the fact that many people have notifications turned on for the IWCB group. While we don’t want to discourage noodling around a certain topic, please be respectful to your fellow community members by keeping the discussion high-signal and relevant to the interests of the group as a whole.
  • In general, avoid posting low-effort responses, tired memes, and links to X, Instagram, and other siloed social media websites.
  • Don’t DM people without asking. Don’t DM in response to something they put on the group or their blog. If you must contact them, use the contact guidelines on their website. If there are no guidelines on their website, feel free to ask them in the group before DMing. The group has a diverse set of people who may not all be comfortable being reached directly.
    • It’s okay to DM somebody if you’ve had significant interactions with them and you have mutually established that it’s okay to reach out.
    • Admins may DM you for them to effectively moderate the group.
    • Remember to add a page on your website with guidelines on how to contact you.
  • Avoid posting links to anything that is hosted on a closed platform, especially if engaging with it requires logging in, e.g. Instagram, Substack social timeline posts, etc. unless no other open accessible sources are available. When in doubt, pleae feel free to message a moderator.

Self-Promotion Guidelines

Dos

  • Before promoting anything in the IWCB chat or community space, make sure you have spent some time engaging with the community. You mainly engage with the community via our in-person meetups, but it’s acceptable if your engagement has been primarily through discussions on the IWCB WhatsApp group.
  • You are encouraged to share links to your writing, photography, art, or other media that you host on your personal blog or website.
  • You can also share links to work you’ve done on your website, or for other websites or publications, but please make sure that work is publicly accessible, about the IndieWeb, IndieWeb-adjacent, or relevant to the ongoing conversation.
  • You are encouraged to promote in-person events you are hosting in Bangalore that might be relevant to the IWCB community. You are also encouraged to promote events you are hosting at Underline Center since we support third spaces for people to gather around. But please make sure these are events you are personally involved in.
  • You are encouraged to promote any open-source software you have written, as long as it’s relevant to IWCB members or relevant to the current discussion.
  • If you really think something your employer has built might be valuable if shared with the IWCB community, please share it. We trust your judgment! But if you share something put out by your employer, please remember to disclose your association.
  • It is okay to share posts on your or other independent blogs that are about selling things or giveaways or community/mutual aid requests.

Don’ts

  • Do not directly upload PDFs, executables, epubs, ZIP files, or other files to the WhatsApp chat. If you must share a file, please share it as a link, preferably hosted on your own personal website.
  • Do not advertise yours or your employer’s commercial products in the community, unless it’s relevant to the discussion. If you do share links to your employer’s products, please disclose the fact that you work for them up-front.
  • In case you’re presenting at one of IWCB’s special events, avoid including links to your or your employer’s products in your presentation unless absolutely relevant. Avoid including employer logos on your slides. Instead, include links to your own personal website/blog. This applies to both the IWCB events and the WhatsApp group.

See also: IWCB Code of Conduct.
