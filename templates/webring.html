Webring

The IndieWebClub Bangalore Webring is a way to connect the websites of its members and encourage discovery within our community. If you're a member, please consider adding the webring navigation to your website.

Adding the Webring to Your Site

The webring forms a circular chain of member websites. Each member has a unique Previous link (pointing to the member before them in the ring) and a unique Next link (pointing to the member after them). The ring order is randomized on each rebuild, so links change periodically.

  1. Follow the instructions in the How to Join section to join our blogroll.
  2. After your pull request is merged and the site rebuilds, enter your URL below to get your embed code.
  3. Add the embed code to your website.

You can customize the appearance of the links with CSS:

.webring {
  border: 1px solid #ccc;
  border-radius: 4px;
  text-align: center;
  font-size: 0.9rem;
  padding: 0.5rem;
}

.webring a {
  text-decoration: none;
  font-weight: bold;
}

.webring a:hover {
  text-decoration: underline;
}

How to Join

  1. Participate in the IndieWebClub Bangalore meetups and join our online community.
  2. Add your blog's feed to the blogroll.opml file with a htmlUrl pointing to your website and webring="true". Your feed must contain at least one post.
  3. Raise a pull request, or contact an admin in the community space.
  4. After your pull request is merged and the site rebuilds, come back to this page, enter your URL, and add the embed code to your website.

You must add the links to your site within a reasonable time, otherwise your website will be removed from the webring (but not from the blogroll).
